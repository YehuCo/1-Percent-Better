# 1% Better App Spec

Nov 9, 2026 · Yehuda

Groups of up to 6 friends hold each other to getting 1% better every day across five categories: Physical, Mental, Spiritual, Social and Skill/Art. Missing a weekly goal gets you clowned, and missing a two-week goal costs you money.

## Goals

The target is 7 check-offs a week, or about one a day, and the two-week goal forces you to mix up your categories.

| Window | Ends | Goal | Total | If you miss it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Sunday night | All 5 at least once, any 2 at least twice | 7 | You get clowned |
| Two-week block | Every second Sunday night, same blocks for the whole group | Any 4 categories at least 3 times, the 5th at least twice | 14 | You pay up |

- **Daily limit:** you can log as much as you want, but only 2 check-offs count toward your goals each day. You pick which 2, and they can be in the same category.
- **Minimum days:** hitting the weekly goal takes at least 4 days.
- **Extra entries:** anything beyond your 2 still shows in the feed and Wrapped.

### Completing the two-week goal

Double up on 2 categories in week 1, then on 2 different categories in week 2.

| Category | Week 1 | Week 2 | Block total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Skill/Art | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Spiritual | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Social | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Mental | 1 | 1 | 2 |

Hitting both weekly goals does not guarantee the block. Doubling the same 2 categories both weeks gives 4, 4, 2, 2, 2, which fails because only 2 categories reached 3.

The app shows what is left. On the Monday of week 2, it tells you which categories still need extra check-offs.

## Groups

A group holds up to 6 people, including you, and people can join until it is full. There is no group size to set.

The group creator sets these when creating the group, and everyone sees them before joining:

- **Cost of a miss:** any amount, including $0 for clowning only
- **How it is charged:** a flat amount or per category missed

Only the group creator can edit these, and they can do it at any time. Each change shows in the feed and starts at the next two-week block, so the stakes never change mid-block.

## Checking off

A check-off should take under 15 seconds: tap a category, pick what you did or write your own, and add a sentence. Then flip "Was this new?" if it was.

**The bar:** did it make you 1% better? Then it counts. The activities below are starting points, not a strict list.

| Category | Examples | Optional detail | Feeds Wrapped |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | Gym, sports, eating better, sleeping well | How long? | Hours moved, top sport |
| Mental | Silence and self-reflection, real conversation | How long? | Minutes in silence |
| Spiritual | Self-reflection, Bible, prayer, making space for the Creator | What passage or theme? | Days in prayer, books read |
| Social | Intentional hangout, meaningful conversation with someone new | Who with? | New people met, top hangout buddy |
| Skill/Art | Reading, creative expression, building a skill or project, instrument, writing, painting, photography | What are you working on? Photo? | Top craft, projects built |

- **One entry, one category:** self-reflection could be Mental or Spiritual, and a real conversation could be Mental or Social. You pick where it goes.
- **Logging window:** you have up to 24 hours to log something and pick that day's 2.
- **No going back:** once a day's 2 are picked, that day locks and cannot be edited. Everything locks when the week closes.
- **Default picks:** if you do not pick, your first 2 entries count.

## Group feed

Everyone's check-offs show up in one feed, where friends can react and comment. Perfect weeks get celebrated there too.

**Private toggle:** anyone can hide their details on Mental and Spiritual entries, so the feed shows only the category.

## When someone slacks

The app warns you twice before the week closes, then the group gets to clown you for an actual miss.

1. **Thursday:** private nudge, such as "Still missing Spiritual and Social. 4 days left."
2. **Saturday:** the group can see you are at risk, and friends can send a nudge.
3. **Sunday night:** the week closes, and any miss shows on the group board.
4. **Clowning opens:** you get a clown on your profile, and the group can leave roast captions.
5. **Redemption:** hit next week's goal and the clown comes off.

Clowning only unlocks after an actual miss, and private details are off-limits for roasts.

### Cap button

If two friends call cap on an entry, you have 24 hours to add proof, or it does not count.

- In a group of 2, one cap is enough.
- Prayer, sitting in silence and self-reflection cannot be capped.
- Other private entries can be capped, but your details stay hidden unless you share them as proof.

## Passes

A pass is a free skip for one day, and the group votes on who gets one.

1. **Request:** you pick the day and give a one-line reason before the week closes. Same-day requests are allowed, and the request shows in the feed.
2. **Vote:** everyone else gets 24 hours to vote yes or no. You do not vote on your own pass, and any clowning or pay-up waits for the result.
3. **Result:** after 24 hours, only the votes cast count. A majority yes gets you the pass, and a tie counts as a no.
4. **If approved:** your weekly and two-week goals each drop by 1 check-off. Your repeat check-offs come off first, then a category you choose.

You can still get clowned or pay up if you miss the lowered goal.

## Pay-ups

Pay-ups happen every 2 weeks, only if someone missed. If everyone hit their goals, no one pays.

- **Who gets paid:** people who missed split their payment evenly among those who hit their goals.
- **If everyone missed:** no money moves, and everyone gets clowned.
- **Tracking:** the app tracks who owes whom. People pay over Venmo, and the person receiving marks it paid.

## Joining late

New members get a prorated goal of one check-off per day left in the block, starting the day after they join.

- Their check-offs show in the feed and Wrapped from day one.
- The prorated goal is their only goal for that block, with no separate weekly goal.
- If they miss it, they get clowned and pay up when the block ends.
- They cannot collect from others until their first full block, when they become a full member.

| Joins on block day | Days left | Goal for that block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | 0 | Nothing, they start fresh the next day |
| 10–13 | 1–4 | One check-off per day left, each in a different category |
| 9 | 5 | All 5 once |
| 8 | 6 | All 5 once, any 1 twice |
| 7 | 7 | All 5 once, any 2 twice |
| 6 | 8 | All 5 once, any 3 twice |
| 5 | 9 | All 5 once, any 4 twice |
| 4 | 10 | All 5 twice |
| 3 | 11 | All 5 twice, any 1 three times |
| 2 | 12 | All 5 twice, any 2 three times |
| 1 | 13 | All 5 twice, any 3 three times |

## Year-end Wrapped

At the end of each year, every member gets a Spotify Wrapped-style recap of their stats in each category.

- **Your year:** total check-offs, perfect weeks, longest run of goals hit and best month
- **Your categories:** strongest, most neglected ("You and Spiritual need to talk"), and top activity in each
- **Your people:** new people met and who you spent the most time with
- **Your yeses:** how many new things you tried, and the best one
- **Your shame:** times clowned, caps called on you, passes denied and dollars paid
- **Your growth:** first month vs. last month, plus your best note from the year
- **Group awards:** Most Consistent, Biggest Comeback, Head Clown and Biggest Earner

## First version

Build a mobile web app that people add to their home screen, which skips the App Store and still supports notifications.

- Group setup, with settings the creator can edit
- Check-offs with the daily 2, locking and the private toggle
- Weekly and two-week progress, including prorated goals
- Group feed and board
- Nudges
- Clowning and Cap
- Pass requests and voting
- Pay-up tracking

Wrapped can wait as long as the data is being collected. Build it this month, test through December, and go live January 1 to capture a full year of data.
